# Business plan research

Read this after `schema.json`. It says where to find each researched field. Research the `market_country`, and write in the `document_language`.

## Market

- `industry`: classify with the official code search in [market-size.md](../../../research/market-size.md).
- `market_analysis`: count the target businesses and find growth and trends with [market-size.md](../../../research/market-size.md). Show the bottom-up math.
- `opportunities` and `threats`: take these from the same market sources and from [competitors.md](../../../research/competitors.md).

## Competition

- `competitors`: list at least three real products found with [competitors.md](../../../research/competitors.md), each with its website.
- `competitive_advantage`: compare the user's context with what those competitors' own pages say.
- `strengths` and `weaknesses`: compare the context with the same competitors.

## Offer and go to market

- `pricing`: use the context, or recommend a price from competitor pages with [pricing.md](../../../research/pricing.md).
- `marketing_plan`: choose channels where the target customer already looks: their trade associations, trade shows, suppliers, and the directories in [competitors.md](../../../research/competitors.md). Give each channel a measure.

## Company and operations

- `legal_structure`, `team`, `starting_capital`: follow [company-facts.md](../../../research/company-facts.md).
- `sourcing` and `operations`: describe how the offer is built, delivered, and supported, with the norms in [company-facts.md](../../../research/company-facts.md).

## Financials

- `funding_request` and `use_of_funds`: use the context, or recommend an amount from cited startup costs in [company-facts.md](../../../research/company-facts.md). Label it `Assumption:`.
- `financials`: build a projection only from cited inputs with [pricing.md](../../../research/pricing.md). Put the customer count and price in Assumptions.

## Sources

- `sources`: one entry per page you opened, with title, publisher, URL, and the date you opened it. If you could not browse, set `research_mode` to `offline`, leave `sources` empty, and label every researched field `Assumption:`.

# Starter research

Replace this with the research steps for the new template. Name every field whose `x-source` is `research`, and link the shared guide it uses.

- `background`: use the sources that fit the topic, such as [market-size.md](../../research/market-size.md).
- `sources`: one entry per page you opened, with title, publisher, URL, and the date you opened it. If you could not browse, set `research_mode` to `offline`, leave `sources` empty, and label every researched field `Assumption:`.

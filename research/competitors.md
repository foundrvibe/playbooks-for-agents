# Competitors

Use this for competitor lists, differences, competitive advantage, and SWOT.

## Where to look

1. **Search the category.** Search for the offer and the customer together, in the document language and in English. For example: "countertop fabrication software" and "logiciel atelier de comptoirs".
2. **Check software directories.** [G2](https://www.g2.com), [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com), and the app marketplace of any platform the customer already uses.
3. **Check the customer's trade.** Industry association vendor directories and trade-show exhibitor lists show who already sells to that customer.
4. **Include the current workaround.** If customers use spreadsheets, messaging apps, or paper today, say so in the market analysis. Do not list a tool as a competitor unless it is a product you can link to.

## What to record per competitor

- Name and public website. Only include a competitor whose website you opened.
- Who it is built for and which countries and languages it supports, from its own pages.
- Its public pricing page, if it has one. See [pricing.md](pricing.md).
- How the user's offer differs. Base this on the competitor's pages and the user's context, not on reviews you did not read.

## Record

Add each competitor page you opened to `sources`.

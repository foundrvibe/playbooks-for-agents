# Company facts

Use this for legal structure, capital, funding, team, and operations.

## Where to look

1. **The user's context.** Use what the user said first.
2. **Public business registries.** Search the company name:
   - Canada: [Corporations Canada](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cc/lgcy/fdrlCrpSrch.html) and the province's registry, such as the [Registraire des entreprises du Québec](https://www.registreentreprises.gouv.qc.ca).
   - United States: the secretary of state business search for the state, and [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) for public filings.
   - United Kingdom: [Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk).
3. **Government startup guides.** For a recommended structure or typical startup costs, use the market country's small-business agency, such as [Innovation Canada](https://innovation.ised-isde.canada.ca) or the [U.S. SBA](https://www.sba.gov).

## Rules

- A legal structure you did not find in the context or a registry is a recommendation. Label it `Assumption:` and cite the guide behind it.
- Capital on hand comes only from the context or a filing. Otherwise give a cited startup-cost estimate, label it `Assumption:`, and say it is not cash the company holds.
- Do not invent people's names. If no one is named in the context or a public source, list the roles the business needs.
- For operations, cite the norms you rely on, such as hosting, support, or delivery practices for this kind of business.

## Record

Add each registry or guide page you opened to `sources`.

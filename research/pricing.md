# Pricing

Use this for price, packaging, and revenue-projection inputs.

## Where to look

1. **The user's context.** A price the user gave wins. Use it as stated.
2. **Competitor pricing pages.** Record the plan name, price, currency, billing unit (per user, per location, per month), and the date you saw it.
3. **When a competitor says "contact sales."** Write that the price is not public. Do not estimate their price.

## How to recommend a price

- Base a recommended price on the cited competitor prices and say which ones.
- Convert to the market country's currency only with a cited exchange rate and its date.
- Label the recommendation `Assumption:` and add it to Assumptions.

## Revenue projections

Build a projection only from inputs you can show: number of customers (an Assumption) × the price (from the context or labeled Assumption). Put both inputs in Assumptions. Do not invent growth rates. Cite any growth rate you use.

## Record

Add each pricing page you opened to `sources`.

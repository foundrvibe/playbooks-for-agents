# Source evaluation

Use this to decide which pages a researched figure or claim may rest on. Research the playbook's `market_country`.

## Rank sources

Prefer the highest rank you can open. Say which one you used.

1. **Official statistics and regulators.** National statistics offices, government registries, and regulator guidance.
2. **Filings and primary company pages.** Annual reports, investor filings, and a company's own pricing or product page.
3. **Industry associations and trade bodies.** Member counts, surveys, and the association's published reports.
4. **Buyer conversations in public.** Reviews, forums, and community threads. Use them for pain and language, not for market figures.
5. **Trade press and blogs.** Use them for trends and leads to better sources, not for the figure itself.

## Rules

- Cite only pages you opened in this run. A page you remember is an assumption.
- Do not use statistics APIs or API keys. Find the same release through web search as an HTML table or PDF on the publisher's site.
- Quote a paid research figure only when the figure is visible on the page you opened.
- Say the year each figure is for. Prefer figures from the last three years.
- Do not add figures that measure different things, such as establishments and firms, or revenue and spending.
- Do not mix countries in one figure.
- When two sources disagree, keep both, say which one you used, and say why.
- Label anything you chose or estimated `Assumption:`, and name the count or page that would replace it.

## Record

Add each page you opened to `sources` with title, publisher, URL, and the date you opened it.

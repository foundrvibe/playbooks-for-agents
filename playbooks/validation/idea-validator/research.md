# Idea check

Read this after `schema.json`. Research the `market_country`, and write in the `document_language`. `template.md` is the result. These steps are the work.

## Steps

1. Ask for `idea`, `target_customer`, and `market_country` when the context does not already answer them. Wait.
2. Write `problem` and `solution_fit`. Urgency has to come from a cited complaint, workaround, or statistic. If you cannot find one, say so. Do not invent urgency.
3. Fill `market` with TAM, SAM, and SOM. Show the math and the page it comes from, using [market-size.md](../../../research/market-size.md). If you did not open a count, label the figure `Assumption:` and show the inputs. Do not present a round number as an official market size.
4. Fill `competitors` from [competitors.md](../../../research/competitors.md). Open each website. Include at least three. Stop at eight. Do not add names to fill a quota. A spreadsheet or paper workaround belongs in `problem`, not in this table. `difference` comes only from that competitor's pages and the user's idea.
5. Fill `community` from pages you opened: reviews, forums, trade discussions, or trend reports. Say what those pages show. If you opened none, say that none were opened. Do not invent influencers, sentiment scores, or a community.
6. Set `decision` from the rows above. **go** only when the problem is cited, at least one competitor gap is cited, and one demand signal is cited. **not yet** when the problem is plausible but the size, the price, or the demand is an assumption. **no** when opened pages show the same offer already sold to the same customer. Write `decision_reason` from those rows.
7. List every estimate in `assumptions` and every page you opened in `sources`.

## Where to look

- `problem` and `solution_fit`: the user's idea, plus the workaround and the gaps in [competitors.md](../../../research/competitors.md).
- `market`: [market-size.md](../../../research/market-size.md). Three rows, in order: TAM, SAM, SOM.
- `competitors`: [competitors.md](../../../research/competitors.md).
- `community`: public discussions and review pages you can open. A directory listing is not a demand signal unless it shows buyers talking about the problem.

## Sources

- `sources`: one entry per page you opened, with title, publisher, URL, and the date you opened it. If you could not browse, set `research_mode` to `offline`, leave `sources` empty, and label every researched field `Assumption:`.
